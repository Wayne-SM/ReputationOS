<objective>
Build the lean business dashboard providing local business owners with the 7 essential MVP metrics, rating distribution breakdown, recent customer feedback feed with source attribution, and business/Google settings management at ₹0 infrastructure cost.
</objective>

<tasks>
1. **Shared Types Expansion**: Update `shared/types/index.ts` with complete `DashboardMetrics`, `DashboardOverviewResponse`, and `BusinessSettingsPayload` contracts.
2. **Dashboard Overview API**: Implement `server/src/routes/dashboard.ts` mounted at `/api/v1/dashboard`. Aggregate the 7 MVP metrics directly via Drizzle ORM queries scoped to `req.business.id`:
   - Reception QR scans (`analytics_events` count for reception page views)
   - Instagram visits (`analytics_events` count for instagram page views)
   - Feedback submissions (`feedback` count)
   - Google review CTA clicks (`analytics_events` count for google clicks)
   - Rating distribution (1 to 5 star counts and percentages)
   - Total feedback count
   - Recent feedback list (latest 20 entries with rating, source badge, customer name, and comment text)
3. **Business Settings API**: Implement `server/src/routes/business.ts` mounted at `/api/v1/business`. Expose `GET /api/v1/business/settings` and `PATCH /api/v1/business/settings` (scoped to `requireRole('admin')`) to update business name, accent color, and Google Review URL.
4. **Backend Route Registration**: Mount `dashboardRouter` and `businessRouter` in `server/src/index.ts`.
5. **Dashboard & Settings Automated Tests**: Author `server/src/routes/__tests__/dashboard.test.ts` to verify metric calculations, rating distributions, tenant scoping isolation, and settings updates.
6. **Frontend Dashboard UI**: Enhance `client/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx` with:
   - Metric summary cards for the 7 numbers
   - Star rating distribution with visual percentage bars and average rating
   - Recent feedback feed with source badges (Reception vs Instagram) and empty states
   - Quick links to Sources and Settings
7. **Frontend Settings Page & Navigation**: Create `client/src/pages/SettingsPage.tsx` for updating business profile and Google Review URL, and integrate `/settings` into top navigation in `App.tsx` and navbar headers.
8. **Verification**: Execute `npm test` in `server`, `tsc --noEmit` / `npm run build` in `client`, and ensure all tests pass.
</tasks>
